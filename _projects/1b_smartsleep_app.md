**CUHK Department of Psychiatry, Li Chiu Kong Family Sleep Assessment Unit · 2026**

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/hk/app/smartsleep/id6761357920?l=en-GB) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cuhk.smartsleep&hl=en)

SmartSleep is the participant-facing app for our adolescent sleep and mood studies. I developed the app, along with the companion back-office system the research team uses to manage participants, questionnaires, and study schedules.

- Daily sleep diaries and mood diaries, including audio and video entries
- Questionnaires and self-assessments organized by study phase, with version matching
- Scheduled reminders that keep participants on track with research tasks
- Offline drafts that sync automatically once the network is back
