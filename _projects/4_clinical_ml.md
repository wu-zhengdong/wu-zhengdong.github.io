**Guangzhou Aid Cloud · 2020 – 2021**

Working with clinicians at tertiary hospitals in China, I applied causal inference and survival analysis to real-world patient data, and designed a reusable interpretable machine-learning framework using SHAP for clinical prediction with transparent model explanations — including a predictor of acute kidney injury after liver transplantation {% cite zhang2021aki %}.
