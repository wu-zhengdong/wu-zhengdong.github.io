**With the Affiliated Brain Hospital of Guangzhou Medical University**

[Tencent App Store (应用宝)](https://sj.qq.com/appdetail/com.cuhk.teenteensleep)

A sleep health app for adolescents, published by the Affiliated Brain Hospital of Guangzhou Medical University with technical development from CUHK. I developed the app and its companion content-management system.

Built on evidence-based strategies from cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I), it provides:

- Sleep diaries and weekly summary reports
- Structured sleep-education courses
- Self-assessment questionnaires
- Relaxation training
- Personalized reminders
