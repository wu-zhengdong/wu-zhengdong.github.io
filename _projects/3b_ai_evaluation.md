**HKU D²4H & CUHK Department of Psychiatry**

Before an AI agent reaches patients or the public, we need to know where it is accurate, where it fails, and how strong the evidence is.

- **Vaccine knowledge.** A benchmark of large language models on multilingual vaccine knowledge {% cite chen2026llmvaccine %}.
- **Mental health agents.** A systematic review and meta-analysis of AI agents for mental health assessment, triage, and psychotherapy support {% cite zhu2026agentsreview %}.
- **Psychiatric reasoning.** An evidence-guided reinforcement learning method that improves psychiatric reasoning in small language models {% cite lin2026psychrl %}.
