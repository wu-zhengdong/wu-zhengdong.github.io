**HKU School of Public Health & D²4H · 2022 – 2025**

I developed multilingual vaccine chatbots — UI/UX, frontend and backend, LLM integration, and cloud deployment — and helped evaluate them in randomized trials.

- **China.** A multi-center cluster randomized trial showed that a chatbot for parents improved HPV vaccination uptake among middle school girls {% cite hou2025chatbot %}. Supported by the Merck Investigator Studies Program (Grant 102194; PI: Dr. Leesa Lin).
- **Japan.** A randomized clinical trial of an AI chatbot for HPV vaccine literacy {% cite kobayashi2026japan %}.
- **Thailand, Hong Kong, and Singapore.** Chatbots to improve COVID-19 vaccine confidence and acceptance {% cite lee2023covidchatbot %}.
