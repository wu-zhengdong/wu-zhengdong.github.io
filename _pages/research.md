I study how to take AI for health from everyday data to clinical evidence. My work follows one research cycle, in which each round of trials and deployment produces the data for the next:

{% include research_cycle.html %}

## Research interests

- Digital phenotyping with wearables and smartphone diaries
- Conversational AI agents for behavior change and digital therapy
- Evaluation of large language models and AI agents in health
- Randomized controlled trials of digital health interventions
- Digital sleep medicine and adolescent mental health
- Vaccine confidence and health misinformation

## The cycle in practice

| Stage        | Vaccine communication (HKU, completed)                                                                                                                                                                    | Sleep and mental health (CUHK, ongoing)                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sense**    | [Social listening](/projects/0_social_listening/) on public perspectives toward mRNA vaccines and health AI                                                                                               | [SmartSleep](/projects/1b_smartsleep_app/) for a 6,000-adolescent cohort, [乐眠无忧](/projects/1c_lemian_app/), and Actigraph, Huawei, and Samsung wearables                                                                      |
| **Model**    | [Multilingual vaccine chatbots](/projects/2_vaccine_chatbots/) and an [AI fact-checking extension](/projects/3_ai_fact_checking/)                                                                         | [AI agents](/projects/1_sleep_ai_agents/) delivering motivational interviewing, imagery rehearsal therapy, and CBT-I; sleep–wake and sleep-stage classification; prediction of adherence and remission in self-help digital CBT-I |
| **Evaluate** | [Benchmark](/projects/3b_ai_evaluation/) of large language models on multilingual vaccine knowledge                                                                                                       | [Systematic review](/projects/3b_ai_evaluation/) of AI agents for mental health; psychiatric reasoning in small language models                                                                                                   |
| **Validate** | Randomized trials in China (_Nature Medicine_), Japan (_JAMA Network Open_), and Thailand, Hong Kong, and Singapore (_npj Digital Medicine_), plus a randomized experiment of the fact-checking extension | Randomized controlled trial of the [motivational interviewing chatbot](/projects/1_sleep_ai_agents/)                                                                                                                              |
